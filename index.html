Full Turnkey Residential

Comprehensive single-point execution from bare shell to final interior styling. We handle civil works, plumbing, bespoke joinery, false ceiling, and acoustic insulation.

  • White-glove deep clean before keys handover
02

Modular Kitchens & Wardrobes

Ergonomically engineered culinary workstations and walk-in wardrobe suites. Fabricated with German CNC precision, anti-scratch acrylics, and synchronized soft-close hardware.

  • Blum & Hettich Lifetime Warranty fittings
  • Natural quartzite & sintered stone surfaces
  • Backlit fluted glass & sensor LED wardrobe systems

3D VR Visualization & Walkthrough

Experience photorealistic 4K architectural renderings and immersive VR simulations. Review exact lighting reflections, marble grain alignments, and scale before procurement.

The Designterio Journey

Our 4-Step Turnkey Methodology

A seamless, transparent journey ensuring your vision is executed on time, within budget, and without the stress of managing vendors.

01

Discover & Scan

We conduct 3D millimeter-accurate laser site scans, analyze natural sun paths, and profile your lifestyle requirements and functional rituals.

02

Design & VR Walkthrough

Our architectural atelier crafts 4K photorealistic renders and full VR models. Touch and approve physical material swatch moodboards in our studio.

03

Manufacture & Civil

Modular joinery is pre-fabricated with German CNC machinery off-site to reduce dust and noise, while our master civil teams execute onsite MEP.

04

Handover & Celebrate

Following a 120-point quality audit, white-glove deep clean, and styling, we deliver keys with comprehensive 10-year warranty documentation.
